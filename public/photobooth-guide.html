Assembly / Montering

1
Approach booth. Stand approximately 1 meter away. Smile optional but recommended. Booth detects E automatically.
2
Camera A captures your photo. Hold still for 1 second. Do your best pose — this is your input to the magic.
3
D (AI Brain) processes your photo through C (GPU Server). Takes ~10 seconds. Your face is transformed into something extraordinary. Style options: knight, astronaut, anime, oil painting, and more.
4
Marvel at your AI portrait on display B. Share with friends. Take a photo of the screen. Post everywhere.
5
Printer F produces a high-quality print of your AI portrait. Take it home. Stick it on the fridge. Frame it.
6
Scan the QR code to find your portrait in the branded online gallery G. Download, share, or show your friends. Each kiosk has its own gallery page. H (Joy) is now activated.
7
BONUS LEVEL: Two portraits are morphed into a video using LTX Video AI. Watch yourself transform frame by frame. Send to smart glasses or share as a keepsake.
